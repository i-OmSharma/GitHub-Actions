An event triggers starts a workflow, and the workflow then runs its defines jobs or tasks.
Within that workflow we have 1 or more jobs each of those job have one or more steps within them and they can be ordered in secipic manner and 
runner is the actual server env that is going to execute the job

Between the steps you are in same compute env.
Within the job you are different compute env.

keep in mind for pasing data, sharing env variables or state between this element. 

workflow is the entire thing 
job is the Object within the workflow
Steps is is the listes within that specific job
